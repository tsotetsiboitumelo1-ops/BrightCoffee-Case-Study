# BrightCoffee-Case-Study
# ☕ BrightCoffee Shop Data Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

The **BrightCoffee Shop Case Study** is a data analytics project focused on analysing coffee shop transaction data to understand **sales performance, customer behaviour, product performance, and revenue trends**.

The project follows a complete data analytics workflow, starting with understanding the business requirements and inspecting the raw data, followed by data cleaning and transformation in **Databricks using SQL**. The cleaned data is then used to identify trends, answer business questions, and develop dashboards and visualisations.

The goal of the analysis is to turn raw transactional data into **actionable business insights** that can support decision-making around products, sales, customers, and overall business performance.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The main objective of this case study is to use transactional data to answer important business questions such as:

* Which products generate the most sales?
* Which products generate the most revenue?
* What are the overall sales and revenue trends?
* Which product categories perform best?
* What are the busiest days and times?
* How does customer purchasing behaviour change over time?
* Which products may require further attention or promotion?
* What patterns can be identified in customer and transaction activity?
* What recommendations can be made based on the data?

---

## 🗂️ Dataset

The dataset contains coffee shop transaction information that can be used to analyse:

* Transaction dates
* Transaction times
* Products
* Product categories
* Quantity sold
* Transaction amounts
* Revenue
* Customer purchasing patterns

The original dataset required data preparation before it could be used effectively for analysis and visualisation.

The cleaned dataset contains **140,000+ transaction records**.

---

## 🔄 Data Analytics Process

The project followed a structured analytics workflow:

### 1. Understanding Business Requirements

The first step was to understand the purpose of the analysis and identify the types of business questions that could be answered using the available data.

### 2. Asking Data-Driven Questions

Business questions were developed around:

* Products
* Revenue
* Sales
* Customers
* Time
* Trends
* Performance

### 3. Data Inspection

The raw data was inspected to understand:

* Available columns
* Data types
* Missing values
* Date and time formats
* Duplicate or inconsistent records
* Potential data quality issues

### 4. Data Cleaning & Transformation

Data preparation was performed in **Databricks using SQL**.

The cleaning process included:

* Converting transaction dates into appropriate date formats
* Extracting useful date components
* Creating transaction year and month fields
* Formatting months for easier reporting
* Creating day-of-week information
* Preparing time-based analysis
* Checking data consistency
* Preparing fields required for analysis and dashboards

### 5. Data Analysis

SQL was used to analyse the cleaned data and identify patterns in:

* Product sales
* Revenue
* Transaction volume
* Customer behaviour
* Time-based performance
* Product performance
* Sales trends

### 6. Data Visualisation

The analysis was transformed into visualisations and dashboards to make the findings easier to understand.

## 🔍 Business Questions

The analysis is designed to answer the following key questions:

### Products

1. What are the best-selling products?
2. Which products generate the most revenue?
3. Which products have the highest demand?
4. Which product categories perform best?
5. Are there products with high sales volume but relatively low revenue?

### Revenue & Sales

6. What is the total revenue?
7. What is the average transaction value?
8. How does revenue change over time?
9. Which periods generate the highest revenue?
10. Are there noticeable changes in sales performance over time?

### Customer Behaviour

11. How many transactions occur during the analysed period?
12. What is the average customer spend per transaction?
13. When are customers most active?
14. What purchasing patterns can be identified?

15. Which month has the highest sales?
16. Which day of the week performs best?
17. What are the busiest times of day?
18. Are there clear peak and low-demand periods?
